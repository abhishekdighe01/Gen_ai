OFFICIAL CFA RULES (MNRE Guidelines Section 5):
1. CFA Structure (Section 5(e) and 5(h)):
   - First 2 kW: ₹30,000 per kW (Total: ₹60,000 for 2 kW)
   - Additional capacity 2-3 kW: ₹18,000 per kW
   - Maximum CFA: ₹78,000 (capped at 3 kW)
   - NO additional CFA beyond 3 kW capacity

2. CFA Calculation Examples:
   - 1.5 kW system: ₹45,000 (1.5 × ₹30,000)
   - 2.5 kW system: ₹69,000 (₹60,000 + 0.5 × ₹18,000)
   - 3.0 kW system: ₹78,000 (₹60,000 + 1.0 × ₹18,000)
   - 4.0 kW system: ₹78,000 (capped at 3 kW equivalent)

3. Eligibility (Section 5(b) and 5(d)):
   - ONLY for grid-connected residential rooftop solar
   - Must be tagged to residential power connection
   - Government/commercial/industrial segments NOT eligible
   - Installations on roofs, terraces, balconies, or BIPV systems

4. DCR Requirement (Section 5(m)):
   - Solar modules MUST be manufactured domestically
   - Must use domestically manufactured solar cells
   - Non-DCR (imported) modules are INELIGIBLE for CFA

5. Capacity Calculation (Section 5(k)):
   - CFA calculated on rated DC capacity of solar modules
   - NOT on inverter capacity
   - Example: 2 kW panels + 3 kW inverter = ₹60,000 CFA (based on 2 kW)

6. Disbursement (Section 5(i)):
   - Two installments:
     * 90% after installation and commissioning
     * 10% after performance report submission
   - Timeline: Typically 15-30 days after DISCOM approval

7. Technical Requirements:
   - Minimum 5-year Comprehensive Maintenance Contract (CMC) warranty
   - 25-year performance warranty on solar modules
   - Net metering mandatory for all installations
   - DISCOM inspection and approval required

8. Space Requirements:
   - Approximately 10 sq m per kW (can vary 9-12 sq m)
   - Depends on panel efficiency and mounting layout
